<objective>
Process the 3 Policy-level essential concepts (ALLOCATION, REGION_BASED_MEMORY_MANAGEMENT, EXECUTION_PROGRAM) and the 2 borderline concepts (CONTEXT_PARAMETERS, REPRESENTATION_MODIFIERS) through the Decision Pipeline.

Purpose: These concepts are not Language or StdLib features — they are Implementation Policy choices (HOW semantics are realized, not WHAT semantics are). EXECUTION_PROGRAM is the core innovation (decoupling semantics from execution strategy). CONTEXT_PARAMETERS and REPRESENTATION_MODIFIERS may require corrections to SEMANTIC_MODEL.md or PRIMITIVE_BLOCKS.md if analysis reveals inconsistencies.

Output: 5 EDRs (3 Policy-classified, 2 Language/StdLib), IMPLEMENTATION_POLICIES.md updates, optional SEMANTIC_MODEL.md/PRIMITIVE_BLOCKS.md corrections.
