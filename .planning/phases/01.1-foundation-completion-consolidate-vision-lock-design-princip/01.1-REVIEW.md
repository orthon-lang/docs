## Summary

Phase 1.1 (Foundation Completion) successfully consolidated the Vision, locked Design Principles, collapsed the Concept Design Review to a 5-step pipeline, finalized process infrastructure documents, and produced an LLM-native concept shortlist. Overall quality is high — the blockquotes in VISION/MANIFESTO/ZEN are consistent in tone, the DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md lock is correctly placed, and the new FITNESS_FUNCTIONS entries follow the existing pattern.

However, two significant issues were found: (1) `CONCEPT_PIPELINE.md` retains stale references to the old 12-step pipeline in its ASCII diagram and supporting text despite the concept-design-review.md having been collapsed to 5 steps, and (2) the `llm-native-concept-shortlist.md` has 5 of 7 cross-references using a `../docs/` prefix that creates broken double-`docs/` paths. Additionally, two planning tracking documents (`.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md` and `.planning/ROADMAP.md`) show Phase 1.1 requirements as "Pending" / "Not started" despite the phase being completed.
